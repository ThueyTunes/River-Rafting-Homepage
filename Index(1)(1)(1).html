Essential Safety Gear Checklist

Your safety is our top priority. We provide all necessary gear, but here is what is mandatory for every rafter before we hit the water:

  • Type V Coast Guard Approved PFD (Personal Flotation Device)
  • Heavy-Duty Whitewater Helmet
  • Neoprene Wet Suit (available for cold-water days)
  • Water Shoes or Closed-Toe Sandals (no flip-flops allowed)
  • Heavy-Duty T-Grip Paddle
